The Colony Guide

How this maps to a real Mars plan

Water ice
Buried ice is common on Mars. Melt it, and you have the feedstock for oxygen and drinking water.
Oxygen from water
Electrolysis splits liquid water into hydrogen and oxygen. A solar panel can run the current by day.
Power and dust
Solar output falls when dust covers the panels and stops at night. Loads have to fit the budget.
Air mixing
A sealed habitat needs fans so oxygen and exhaled CO2 do not pool in corners.
Pressure shell
Air inside pushes out on every seam. Ring tests, pressure gauges, and patches are permanent jobs.
