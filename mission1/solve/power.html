Power Budget

Your solar panel makes only a little power. Choose what matters most, then deal with dust and night.

How to play

Use the up and down arrows, or drag a row, to put the devices in order. Then press Check order.

Next, each device has a switch. Tap it to turn it on or off. The bar shows how much power you are using.

If dust covers the panel, tap Clean the panel. Then press Run it.
