Make Enough Oxygen

Two ants, two helmets, melted ice, and a solar panel.

How to play

Electricity splits water into hydrogen and oxygen.

Tap each oxygen bubble, labeled O2, to send it into the active helmet tank.

Let the hydrogen bubbles, labeled H2, rise and escape. Every O2 made comes with exactly two H2.
