Ring Test

A way to ask a pressure shell if it is intact.

How to play

First learn two sounds: sealed rings clear and long; cracked is dull and short.

Watch the ripple bar under each panel. Long means sealed. Short means a crack.

Then try it yourself. Replay Sealed and Cracked anytime from the buttons at the top.
