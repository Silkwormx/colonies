The vision

Colonies is an illustrated story about ants building a home on Mars, one problem at a time. Six missions: Can't Breathe, Too Cold, Radiation, No Soil, Dust Storm, and The Dome. The series builds toward a lush banyan world under glass.

How ANTS helps

  • Supporter contributions pay for art and writing for new missions.
  • Each mission ends with a thank-you to everyone who fed the colony.
  • Supporters may cast an advisory vote on the next Mars problem. The creator decides. Votes are not weighted by holdings.
  • A new mission goes live when it's finished — never on token price, volume, or holdings.

The Colony Guide · What a token is

A token is a small digital IOU that fits in a pocket that does not exist. It is not a sandwich. It will not keep you warm on Mars. It may, however, help mark who showed up for the nest.
