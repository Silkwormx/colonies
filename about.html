About Colonies

The idea

Why Colonies

Space is amazing, but learning how life on Mars would actually work usually feels like homework. And the fun stuff, like shows and games, rarely explains anything real.

Colonies turns real Mars science into a story worth following. Two ants try to make a home on an empty planet, one problem at a time. You read their story, then solve the same problem they’re facing. It’s fun first, and you learn without noticing.

The problem

Most people are curious about Mars and space, but the ways to learn about it tend to be either dry or shallow. Textbooks and articles feel like homework. Most space shows and games are just for fun and don’t explain how anything would actually work.

Where Colonies fits

Colonies sits in the middle. It’s a story you follow because you care about two ants. Along the way, you find out how you’d really make air, stay warm, block radiation, grow food and survive a dust storm on Mars, and you solve each problem yourself in a quick hands-on game. It never feels like learning, but you come away understanding something real.
